01 ABOUT

Curiosity first.
Build, test, learn.

I’m most interested in the point where physics, engineering, and real-world testing meet. Rather than stopping at theory, I like understanding why a system works, turning that idea into something testable, and improving it from the results.

My current work spans small spacecraft systems, materials research, and astronomical data analysis. I’m continuing to learn the mathematics, programming, and experimental skills needed for aerospace R&D.
